1. **Basement, no local pages.** "basement clean out near me" (26 imp, position
   81.8, new) and "basement cleanout near me" (22 imp, position 81.1, new)
   appeared this week. Position 81 on a "near me" query means Google has no local
   basement page to serve. /services/basement-cleanouts was not in
   COMBO_SERVICE_SLUGS, so zero area pages existed for it.

2. **"best {service} {area}" is a real query pattern now.** Three separate
   queries: "best junk removal south hills" (8.0), "best mattress removal south
   hills" (9.3), "best basement cleanout pittsburgh" (11.2). No page answers a
   "best" question anywhere on the site. This is also GEO_RULES target query
   "best estate cleanout company Pittsburgh", and comparison-shaped content is
   the format AI engines cite most.
